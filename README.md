<h2 align="center">Brief Info</h2>

Hello! I am a PhD student (doctorant) at Aix Marseille Université in France, since October 2024, supported by <a href="https://mathphdinfrance.fr">COFUND</a>. My supervisors are Colin Guillarmou and Rémi Rhodes, and I expect to defend my thesis by June 2027. Before that, I obtained my master's degree at Fudan University in China, supervised by Elie Aïdékon.

Email: yang.xiao@univ-amu.fr

<h2 align="center">Research Interests</h2>

My research interests are in mathematical physics, especially conformal field theory, which I study with probabilistic, geometric and algebraic tools. In particular, I work on <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.18226">compactified imaginary Liouville theory</a>. Before that, I worked on random walks on trees.
